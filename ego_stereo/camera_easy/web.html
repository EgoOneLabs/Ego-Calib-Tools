Calibration order and prerequisites

Recommended order: Camera → IMU → Camera–IMU. Camera and IMU can be done in either order; joint motion can be recorded first. Joint calculation needs camera parameters, IMU noise parameters, target geometry and a usable timeline from the same headband assembly. Optional joint-result review uses a separate recording. Read the reports before accepting any result.

Before you start

For long-term noise analysis, aim for at least 2–3 hours of stationary data. You can adjust the duration; short records may not reveal long-term noise. Recording starts immediately.

Two parameter files for joint calculation (can be added later)

Recording does not require parameter files. Joint calculation needs the two YAML files below, not PDF or TXT reports. Parameters generated or explicitly used for this device may be restored automatically; check their names. Experimental time mapping and noise weights require review against the reports.

Result files

Calculation and file checks are complete. For Camera, inspect reprojection errors and target coverage. For IMU, inspect continuity, stationarity and Allan curves. Generated files alone do not establish calibration quality.
